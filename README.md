# AI Adoption in Higher Education – A TAM-Based Statistical Study

## Overview

This project investigates the acceptance and use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies among students in higher education, based on the **Technology Acceptance Model (TAM)**.

The study aims to understand the factors influencing students' acceptance of AI tools through questionnaire data and statistical analysis.

## Theoretical Framework: Technology Acceptance Model (TAM)

The analysis focuses on two key dimensions:

- **Perceived Usefulness (PU):** The extent to which students believe that AI tools improve their learning and academic performance.
- **Perceived Ease of Use (PEOU):** The extent to which students perceive AI tools as easy to learn and use.

These dimensions provide a framework for understanding students' perceptions and acceptance of AI technologies.

## Methodology

1. **Data Collection:** Collect responses through questionnaires and interviews.
2. **Data Preprocessing:** Clean, transform, and prepare the dataset using Python.
3. **Descriptive Analysis:** Examine students' profiles, AI usage patterns, and questionnaire responses.
4. **TAM-Based Analysis:** Analyze perceived usefulness and perceived ease of use.
5. **Statistical Analysis:** Explore relationships between variables using appropriate statistical methods.
6. **Data Visualization:** Present key indicators and findings through Power BI dashboards.
